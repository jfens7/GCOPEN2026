Terms & Conditions

Please read and scroll to the bottom to agree.

1. Acceptance of Terms
By registering for the 2026 Gold Coast Open Table Tennis Championships, you agree to abide by the rules and regulations set forth by the Gold Coast Table Tennis Association (GCTTA) and Table Tennis Queensland (TTQ).

2. Eligibility
All players must hold a valid National Member ID. If you do not have a rating, you must enter 'Never Played' or select a comparable grade.

3. Media Release
By entering, you grant GCTTA the right to take photographs and video during the tournament for promotional purposes.

4. Refunds
Withdrawals made after the closing date will not be eligible for a refund. Extenuating medical circumstances require a certificate.

5. Equipment and Attire
All equipment must comply with ITTF regulations. White clothing is strictly prohibited during gameplay as the official ball is white.

6. Conduct
Good sportsmanship is expected at all times. The Referee's decision on all matters is final.

7. Late Registration & Manual Payment Fees
Registrations made after 5:00 PM on Wednesday the 5th of August shall incur a flat $10 admin fee. Furthermore, manual payments made on the day of the tournament will incur a $10 manual processing fee per event.
